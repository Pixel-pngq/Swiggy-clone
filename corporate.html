Our story

Swiggy started in 2014 with a simple idea: make it effortless for people to get the things they need, delivered reliably to their doorstep. What began as a food delivery service in Bengaluru has grown into one of India's largest on-demand delivery platforms, now spanning food, groceries, pickups and dining across hundreds of cities.

Behind every order is a network of restaurant partners, delivery partners and technology built to make convenience simple, fast and dependable — every single day.
